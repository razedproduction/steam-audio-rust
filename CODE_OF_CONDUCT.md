# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful place to work on spatial audio and Rust.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith; ask questions before assuming mistakes were careless.
- Give credit for others' work, including the original Steam Audio authors.
- Keep discussion focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberate derailing.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block participants who violate these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through the contact details on the [razedproduction](https://github.com/razedproduction) profile.
